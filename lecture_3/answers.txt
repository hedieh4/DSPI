6. Screen Resolution
My normal screen resolution is 1920 x 1080 and my normal display orientation is Landscape.
When I changed the display orientation to Portrait, the screen rotated by 90 degrees. The desktop became vertically oriented, making the screen taller and narrower.
I then changed the orientation back to Landscape.
When I changed the screen resolution to 800 x 600, there was much less usable screen space. Text, icons, menus, and windows appeared larger, and less information could fit on the screen at the same time.
After testing, I changed the resolution back to 1920 x 1080.
